About the matrix

The matrix gives the learning design team a shared, transparent picture of what is expected at each level and how to grow. It is a guide for conversations, not a checklist.

Using it in your Progress Plan

Progress Plan Review sectionHow the matrix helps
Employee information – CDF levelADLD and DLD are CDF Level 1. SDLD and LDLD are CDF Level 2.
Looking back – overall performanceCompare achievements with your role's descriptors to agree your current level for each competency. Mission and values are reflected here.
Looking forward – goals and development planTake a goal from the next descriptor up, and use the competency's CDF tag in the 'Linkage to CDF' column. The plan builder does this for you.
Looking forward – development plan and next stepsRecord the target descriptor as the development and the linked LinkedIn Learning path (or other PD) as the agreed action.
Looking forward – career growth and aspirationThe next role's Commencing descriptors show where to aim.
Position description reviewUse the matrix to check whether your PD still reflects your role.
Regular check-insRevisit one or two competencies at each catch-up rather than the whole matrix.

Note: the 'Linkage to CDF' dropdown in the Progress Plan template lists nine capabilities and does not include Live ACU's mission, vision and values. Record progress on the Mission and values competency in the Looking Back section.
